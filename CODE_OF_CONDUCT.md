# Code of Conduct

Nila Webs contributors are expected to keep project discussions respectful, technical and constructive. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, or deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

When reviewing code, critique the implementation rather than the person. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

Security reports containing sensitive details must follow `SECURITY.md` instead of being posted publicly.
